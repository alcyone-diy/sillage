FAQ & Troubleshooting

Why did Track Recording or Anchor Watch stop in the background?

To compute continuous GPS fixes while your screen is locked, iOS requires Location Services set to "While Using the App" or "Always" with "Precise Location" turned ON under Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Sillage.

When a Track Recording or Anchor Watch session is active, Sillage maintains a background location session (indicated by the blue location pill in the iOS status bar). Do not force-close (swipe up) the app from the iOS App Switcher while a session is running.

Why is the 7-Day Barograph not showing pressure data?

Sillage reads the internal iPhone hardware barometer via CoreMotion, which requires "Motion & Fitness" permission enabled under Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness > Sillage (as well as the global Fitness Tracking toggle enabled in Motion & Fitness).

Note that iOS does not store historical barometric data prior to launching the app; the 7-day rolling pressure chart and 3-hour trends build progressively while Sillage runs in the foreground or updates in the background.

How do I access official hydrographic charts (GeoGarage) or import MBTiles?

Sillage does not sell chart subscriptions or in-app purchases. All navigation instruments, GPX logging, anchor watch, barometer, and checklists work without any account.

To use official hydrographic office raster charts (SHOM, UKHO, NOAA, etc.), connect an existing third-party GeoGarage account via secure OAuth in Settings > Chart Preferences to stream or download offline packages. Alternatively, you can import standalone .mbtiles chart files directly from the iOS Files app.

Will the Anchor Watch alarm sound if my iPhone is in Silent Mode?

Yes. When the Anchor Watch siren triggers, Sillage activates a dedicated high-priority audio playback session (AVAudioSession playback category with background audio) that overrides the physical Silent/Ring switch, accompanied by a full-screen visual alert and continuous vibration.

However, you must ensure your device's media volume is turned up, allow Notifications in iOS Settings, and keep Sillage running in the background without force-closing it while anchored.

How do I backup or export my recorded tracks?

All navigation data is stored locally on your device in an embedded SQLite database. You can export any recorded track as a standard .gpx file from the Tracks detail view using the native iOS Share Sheet (to save to iCloud Drive / Files, AirDrop, or import into other charting software).

Overview & Features

Engineered by sailors for coastal and offshore passages, Alcyone Sillage combines official raster charts, continuous GPX track logging, an intelligent anchor watch, a 7-day barograph, and interactive safety checklists in a distraction-free interface—with zero telemetry or tracking.

Charts & Offline Navigation

  • Official Charts via GeoGarage: Connect your existing GeoGarage account via secure OAuth to stream or download official hydrographic office raster charts (SHOM, UKHO, NOAA, etc.) for 100% offline use at sea.
  • Interactive Offline Regions: Select custom chart areas on the map with real-time surface calculation (NM²) and background downloading.
  • Local MBTiles Import: Import your own .mbtiles chart files directly from the iOS Files app.
  • Camera & Vectors: Navigate in Free, North Up, or Course Up modes. Includes a visual GPS accuracy halo, a configurable Rhumb Line COG Predictor vector (10 min to 6h with time ticks), and a basic OpenSeaMap seamark layer.

Telemetry HUD & Chart Tools

  • Customizable Telemetry HUD: Long-press the top bar to configure wave-damped SOG and COG (4s smoothing), Bearing to Waypoint (BTW), Range (RNG), Cross-Track Error (XTE), and battery level.
  • Two-Point Divider Tool: Drop two pins on the chart to measure exact Great Circle bearing, reciprocal bearing, and distance.
  • 100% Offline Almanac & Variation: Tap any point to compute local magnetic declination (NOAA WMM-2025 model) and daily solar ephemerides (Nautical Dawn, Sunrise, Sunset, Nautical Dusk via USNO algorithms).
  • Waypoints & Go-To: Create waypoints via the visual crosshair or DMM coordinates, trigger active Go-To routing, and monitor XTE.

Continuous GPX Track Recording

  • Background Logging: Record your vessel's wake accurately with the screen locked. Tracks are continuously persisted to local SQLite storage to survive background termination.
  • Statistics & Export: Review distance (WGS-84), duration, Max/Avg SOG, add logbook notes, and export standard .gpx files.

Intelligent Anchor Watch

  • Drop & Arm Workflow: Drop the anchor point at the bow, veer chain while watching real-time distance, or manually adjust the anchor position via the bearing/range crosshair before arming.
  • Multi-Trigger Safety Alerts: Plots your vessel's swing track and triggers a full-screen visual and audible siren if the vessel exceeds the radius, if GPS accuracy degrades, or if the GPS signal is lost—protected by a Slide-to-Disarm lock.

7-Day Barograph & Weather Alarm

  • Hardware Barometer: Uses the internal pressure sensor with a 2-minute moving average to filter wave noise, plus custom sea-level calibration (-50 to +50 hPa).
  • 7-Day History & Drop Alerts: Scrub through a 7-day rolling pressure chart with 3h trends. Configure alerts for Squalls (-2.0 hPa/1h), Gales (-3.0 hPa/3h), or Storms (-5.0 hPa/3h).

Marine Checklists

  • Ready-to-Use & Custom Templates: Includes Pre-Departure, Anchoring, MOB, and Heavy Weather checklists, plus custom template creation.
  • Geotagged Audit Log: Checking off each step automatically records the exact UTC time and GPS coordinates.

Notes on Accounts & Battery

  • No Account Required for Core Features: All instruments, GPX tracking, MBTiles import, anchor watch, barometer, and checklists work without any account. Accessing GeoGarage charts requires an existing third-party GeoGarage subscription (Sillage does not sell subscriptions or charts).
  • Background GPS & Battery: Continued use of GPS running in the background during Track Recording or Anchor Watch can significantly decrease battery life.

Data & Privacy Policy

Sillage is built on an on-device, privacy-by-design architecture:

  • Zero Analytics or Tracking: Sillage collects zero analytics, zero crash telemetry via third-party SDKs, and no advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • Local Storage Only: Your GPS tracks, waypoints, anchor watch logs, barometric history, and checklists are stored exclusively on your device. Sillage does not operate backend tracking servers or collect personal telemetry.
  • Location & Sensor Data: GPS coordinates and hardware sensor data (barometer, compass) are processed locally in real time strictly for navigation display, track recording, weather alerts, and anchor watch evaluation.
  • Third-Party Chart Providers: When fetching online tiles (OpenSeaMap or GeoGarage), standard HTTPS tile requests are made directly to those providers. GeoGarage OAuth tokens are stored exclusively inside the encrypted iOS Keychain.
